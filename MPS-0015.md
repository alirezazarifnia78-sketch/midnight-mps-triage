# Triage: MPS-0015, Agent identity gap on Midnight

**Source:** [mps-0015-agent-identity.md](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-improvement-proposals/blob/main/mps/mps-0015-agent-identity.md)
**Category:** Standards · **Status:** Proposed · **Created:** 18 May 2026

## Summary

AI agents are starting to operate on Midnight, and there's no standard way for them to state who they are, build a reputation or be validated by others. Without one, teams will build incompatible identity schemes. The MPS contrasts this with ERC-8004 on EVM chains, where agent metadata and reputation are fully public, which is a problem for agents that need to keep their strategy private.
