# Triage: MPS-0039, Calling a contract requires its full compiled artifacts

**Source:** [mps-0039-lightweight-contract-interaction.md](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-improvement-proposals/blob/main/mps/mps-0039-lightweight-contract-interaction.md)
**Category:** Libraries and Tooling · **Status:** Proposed · **Created:** 9 Sep 2026 · **Linked MIP:** MIP-0020

## Summary

To call a Midnight contract, a client must hold the contract's full compiled output: circuit executables plus proving and verification artifacts. These are large (prover keys range from 14 KB to 72.9 MB per circuit; ten small test contracts total 384 MB), must be kept in sync with every contract upgrade, and have no lightweight equivalent of an Ethereum ABI. The burden falls hardest on wallets, agent CLIs, signers and back-end services, and it multiplies with contract-to-contract calls.

## 1. Complete?

Mostly. The problem, use cases, goals and open questions are strong. Acknowledgements are a placeholder, and there's no dedicated security and privacy section, even though delegating work to another service raises privacy questions.
