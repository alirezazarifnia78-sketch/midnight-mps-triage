# Midnight MPS triage

My triage of three Midnight Problem Statements (MPS) from [midnightntwrk/midnight-improvement-proposals](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-improvement-proposals), written while preparing for the Junior Product Manager role at the Midnight Foundation.

For each MPS I checked:

1. **Completeness:** are the template sections really filled in?
2. **Clarity:** could someone decide on it without asking the author?
3. **Duplicates:** does it overlap existing MPSs or MIPs?
4. **Importance:** a score against [my rubric](RUBRIC.md).
5. **Recommendation:** move forward, send back, merge or park.

| MPS | Title | Score | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [0039](MPS-0039.md) | Calling a contract requires its full compiled artifacts | 12 / 14 | Prioritise |
| [0025](MPS-0025.md) | Source-of-funds evidence for incoming shielded assets | 10 / 14 | Move forward after small fixes |
| [0015](MPS-0015.md) | Agent identity gap | 6 / 14 | Send back: split the problem from the solution |

I come from fintech product management (Open Banking, KYC/KYB, AML), not from blockchain, so I've focused on problem quality and on the regulated-privacy angle.

Drafted with AI assistance, then checked against the source documents and edited by me. I'm accountable for the content, the same rule MIP-0001 sets for proposal authors.
